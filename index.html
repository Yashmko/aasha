A short, honest story

This one's personal. My mum checks on her mother once a day by phone. Some days she's fine, some days she forgets her BP tablet and doesn't mention it. Some days she's dizzy and doesn't think it matters.

I built AASHA so that single daily call always happens — and so we finally hear what's really going on, instead of the "I'm fine" we've all learnt to say.

How it works

A daily call, not a chatbot.

Six small things happen on every call. That's the whole product — done well.

01
📞

It rings, on time

AASHA calls every day at a fixed time — morning or evening — whichever the family picks. No app, no setup on the elder's side.

02
🗣️

It speaks their language

हिन्दी, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, English — AASHA detects and replies in whatever the elder speaks naturally, so the call feels human.

03
💊

It asks about meds

It gently checks whether today's tablet was taken — and catches it when the answer is a quiet “oh, I forgot”.

04
⚠️

It notices red flags

Dizziness, a fall, chest pain, not eating, loneliness. Real signals, not templates — AASHA tells the difference between “I'm fine” and “I'm not”.

05
📱

It texts you

If something seems off, the family gets a message immediately: what was said, why it was flagged, and a suggested next step.

06
🩺

It preps a doctor note

Each call becomes a short, shareable clinical summary — mood, meds taken, flags, and concerns — ready to hand to a doctor.
