## 🗣️ 1-min elevator pitch (word-for-word)

> **Problem:** One in six elderly people live alone, and a missed daily dose or an undetected fall at 3am can spiral into an emergency. Their families — often in another city or country — don't have time to call every day.

> **AASHA's fix in one sentence:** AASHA is an **agentic, multilingual AI voice companion** that phones your ageing parent, checks mood and medication, catches red flags, alerts the family, and drafts a doctor-ready note — all in the parent's own language, on any ordinary phone, no app to install.

> **Why us:** Three things most of these tools don't do. **One, it's truly multilingual** — it auto-detects Hindi, Punjabi or English and replies in the same language, because a 75-year-old won't use an English bot. **Two, it's agentic and remembers** — it keeps context across calls, recalls a grandchild or a condition, and knows today's medication schedule. **Three, it escalates** — it doesn't just chat; it flags dizziness, falls, chest pain or a missed critical dose and notifies the family *and* hands a doctor a clean clinical summary.

> **Ask / vision:** We want to give every family a way to feel safe about the people they love — starting with the day AASHA makes *one call that catches a problem before it becomes a crisis.*

*(That's ~135 words ≈ 50–55 seconds spoken.)*

## 💰 Business model

- **Primary buyer:** adult children / NRIs who want peace of mind about their parents.
- **Model:** a low monthly subscription (e.g. **₹299/mo** per parent, or a family plan); near-zero marginal cost per call.
- **Vectors to scale:** partnerships with **eldercare communities, senior-living homes, and health insurers** (who have a direct incentive to keep members medicine-compliant and out of the ER).
- **Why it's compelling:** one caught fall or one caught missed dose can offset years of subscription cost — a strong, provable ROI.
